Kasperia Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 13, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kasperia handles information when you use the Kasperia mobile application and related wallet features. Kasperia is a self-custodial wallet focused on the Kaspa ecosystem.

By using Kasperia, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with it, please stop using the app.

1. Self-Custodial Wallet

Kasperia is a self-custodial wallet. Wallets are created or imported on your device, and you control the private keys and recovery phrase associated with your wallet.

Kasperia does not intentionally collect, store, or request your private keys, recovery phrases, wallet passwords, or biometric information. Kasperia cannot recover a lost recovery phrase, reset a wallet password, reverse a blockchain transaction, or access assets held at your wallet addresses.

2. Information Processed on Your Device

To provide wallet functionality, Kasperia may store information locally on your device, including:

  • Encrypted wallet and account data;
  • Wallet addresses, transaction records, address labels, and selected network settings;
  • Application preferences, language settings, and security settings; and
  • Data required to connect to supported wallet, QR-code, or authentication features.

This local data is used to operate the app. Kasperia does not use it for advertising or behavioral profiling.

3. Public Blockchain Information

Blockchain networks are public. When you view a balance, inspect a transaction, or submit a transaction, Kasperia may request or display public information such as wallet addresses, balances, transaction hashes, token identifiers, block information, network status, and transaction fees.

Information recorded on a blockchain may be permanent and may be visible to anyone. Kasperia does not control blockchain records and cannot delete or alter them.

4. Network Requests and Technical Information

Kasperia connects to blockchain nodes, explorers, remote configuration services, and other infrastructure required for the app to function. Those services may receive technical information that is normally transmitted when a device connects to the Internet, such as an IP address, device or operating-system information, request time, application version, and error information.

Kasperia does not intentionally use these requests to identify you personally, sell personal information, or build an advertising profile. Independent service providers may process information under their own policies.

5. Camera, Photos, and Device Permissions

Kasperia may request camera access to scan wallet addresses, payment requests, or other QR codes. Photo-library access may be requested to import a QR-code image or save a QR code, depending on the feature you use.

These permissions are used only for the related feature. You can manage them in your device settings. If you deny a permission, the corresponding feature may not be available.

6. Third-Party Services, DApps, and Links

Kasperia may provide links or connection tools for third-party websites, decentralized applications, blockchain nodes, explorers, bridge services, and other Web3 services. These services are not operated or controlled by Kasperia.

When you choose to connect to a third-party service, that service may receive your public wallet address, transaction or network information, connection information, or other information you choose to provide. The third party’s own privacy policy and terms apply to that interaction. Review them before connecting or approving a request.

7. Analytics, Advertising, and Sale of Information

Kasperia does not intentionally use wallet information or public blockchain information for targeted advertising or behavioral profiling. Kasperia does not sell your personal information.

Third-party websites and services opened from the app may use their own analytics, advertising, cookies, or tracking technologies. Their practices are outside this Privacy Policy.

8. Security

Kasperia uses reasonable technical measures to protect the app and locally stored wallet data. No device, software, network, or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

You are responsible for securing your device, installing the app from an authorized source, keeping your recovery phrase offline and private, avoiding phishing sites, and reviewing every transaction before approval.

9. Data Retention and Deletion

Wallet and preference data stored locally remains on your device until you remove it, clear the app’s data, or delete the app, subject to the behavior of your operating system and backups.

Public blockchain records cannot be removed by deleting Kasperia. Information processed by third-party services is subject to their retention policies.

10. Children’s Privacy

Kasperia is not directed to children. Kasperia does not knowingly request personal information from children through the app.

11. Privacy Rights and Requests

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding personal information processed by a service provider, including rights to access, correction, deletion, restriction, or objection.

Because Kasperia is self-custodial and does not maintain a user account database or custody wallet credentials, some requests may not apply to information that exists only on a public blockchain or on your device.

Where applicable, privacy questions or requests may be submitted through the official support channel or website associated with Kasperia.

12. Changes to This Policy

Kasperia may update this Privacy Policy when the app, services, or applicable requirements change. The latest version will be published at the policy link provided in the app. The date at the top of this page shows when the policy was last updated.
